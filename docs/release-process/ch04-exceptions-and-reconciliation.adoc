[[ch04]]
= Exceptions, reconciliation and historical record
:description: Temporary exceptions, accepted architectural limitations, documentation reconciliation and the historical record of superseded requirements.

*Scope:* Existing exceptions, accepted architectural limitations, reconciliation of historical documentation and preservation of superseded architectural decisions.

*Status:* Adopted (revision 4). Existing approved decisions remain applicable. Exception dispositions marked "proposed" require confirmation before implementation or extension of their scope.

This chapter distinguishes between:

* Temporary exceptions to established requirements.
* Explicitly accepted architectural or operational limitations.
* Required reconciliation of existing documentation.
* Historical requirements that have been superseded.

No temporary exception becomes permanent policy through repeated use or lapse of time.

An exception accepted for staging does not automatically apply to production.

An exception must remain identifiable, limited in scope and attributable to its original authorization.

The historical sections of this chapter are not normative.

== Accepted architectural and operational limitations

The following limitations arise from explicitly established architectural or organizational decisions.

They are not unresolved implementation defects merely because a more restrictive architecture could eliminate them.

Their scope and consequences must nevertheless remain visible.

[cols="1,3,2,4",options="header"]

| EXC-08
| Review and approval through two maintainer accounts does not necessarily constitute independent review by two individuals (S3; DEC-16).
| Operations performed under the existing maintainer arrangement.
| Document the actual review arrangement accurately.

Do not claim independent review where it does not exist.

Existing repository review, authorization and protected-environment approval requirements remain applicable.

Reassess if the governance or credential-custody arrangements change.

Any production requirement for independent security review remains subject to the applicable production acceptance policy.

| EXC-13
| Horizontal staging source discrepancy.

The APK produced by Magic and the IPA produced by the development-to-staging promotion may carry identical application version and build-number information while originating from different application source revisions.
| Staging delivery under the approved target architecture.
| Explicitly accepted by the CTO through DEC-33.

Compare the relevant application source and build inputs.

Report and preserve detected differences in the workflow summary and durable delivery evidence (`DEL-05`).

Source differences alone do not prohibit staging delivery.

Missing or unverifiable artifact evidence remains a delivery blocker.

Production APK, AAB and IPA must originate from the same approved application source.

The staging validation evidence required for production authorization remains subject to UNR-06.

The previously proposed `config/release/consumed-builds.json` is no longer a mandatory architectural component.

Its intended guarantees remain applicable:

* Previously consumed numbers cannot be allocated again.
* Allocation history remains durable and verifiable.
* Failed and interrupted attempts remain identifiable.
* Safe historical initialization is established before operational use.

These guarantees are governed by DEC-37, DEC-38, `VER-13` and `VER-14`.

A complete historical register, verified numerical boundaries or another sufficiently reliable mechanism may satisfy the initialization requirements.

The precise storage implementation remains subject to technical investigation.

Retired requirement and work-package identifiers must not be reassigned to unrelated requirements.

== Staging recovery decisions R1 to R3

The recovery decisions of 2026-11-27 established the operational response to the unsuccessful staging delivery.

Their principal conclusions were:

* A failed authorized delivery is not re-run using the same consumed build number.
* Recovery requires a new build number and a new written authorization.
* Temporary Apple credentials are provisioned before the promotion merge.
* Credential removal and authorization withdrawal follow delivery.

The historical rationale is preserved in:

* `docs/release-records/ios-staging-authorization.md`, section 3.
* Issue #71, comment 7218186057.

Their current architectural treatment is established by DEC-13, DEC-24 and DEC-28.

Magic replaces the earlier manual advancement mechanism once the approved target architecture becomes operational.

The staging recovery procedure does not independently resolve partial production delivery and recovery.

That question remains subject to UNR-09.

== Exception management

Every continuing temporary exception must identify:

* The requirement or guarantee affected.
* The reason for accepting the exception.
* Its applicable environment, artifact or operational scope.
* The conditions under which the exception remains valid.
* The proposed remediation or closure condition.
* The authorization supporting its acceptance.

An exception must be reassessed when its underlying conditions materially change.

Closing an exception requires either:

* Demonstrated remediation of the underlying problem.
* Removal of the circumstances that required the exception.
* An explicit architectural decision replacing the affected requirement.

Closing an exception does not erase its historical record.

Accepted architectural limitations must remain documented wherever they affect operational decisions or security claims.

Production release authorization must identify and evaluate all applicable outstanding exceptions and accepted limitations.

No staging exception is inherited automatically merely because production uses the staging infrastructure as its architectural reference.
