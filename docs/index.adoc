== About this documentation

This is the entry point of the documentation of the Cadena Signer, a non-custodial Flutter application (Android and iOS) that signs Discreet Log Contract transactions for the DLCPlaza backend. The documentation describes how a reviewed change becomes a verified build, and how a release is authorized, signed and delivered.

It keeps three things apart:

* *What is decided and required.* The adopted release-framework specification: xref:{specdir}index.adoc#ch01[open the specification book].
* *What exists today.* The as-built overview (`STA-01`), in this book.
* *How to write and check documentation.* The conventions, in this book.

The documents under `docs/` that are not yet migrated stay where they are and remain in force for current behavior. They are listed below. Migration into the hierarchy of Chapter 6 is step P8 of the specification.
